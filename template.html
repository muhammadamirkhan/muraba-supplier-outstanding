Where each figure comes from

Every figure on this page is read from Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (company Muraba Properties LLC, Production environment) each time the page is refreshed. Nothing is keyed in, cached or held in a spreadsheet. The tables below give the BC source and the derivation for each metric.

Balances

Outstanding / Balance PendingVendor Ledger Entries — Remaining Amount (LCY)The sum of the unapplied balance of every entry for that supplier, sign-reversed for display. This is the AP subledger balance: what is left open after applications. A figure in red brackets is a credit balance — the supplier owes us.
InvoicedVendor Ledger Entries — Amount (LCY), credit sideAmounts charged to us, less credit memos (which reduce the charge rather than count as settlement). Taken from the signed amount rather than the Debit/Credit columns, because BC books certain reversing journals with negative debits and negative credits.
PaidVendor Ledger Entries — Amount (LCY), debit sideAmounts settled, excluding credit memos. Covers payments, refunds and journal settlements — not only documents typed as Payment, since roughly half of BC’s AP entries are journal-posted with no document type.

Payment performance

FigureSource in Business CentralDerivation
Avg Days to Pay · On-Time Rate · Within 60 Days · Payments AnalysedVendor Ledger Entries — Posting Date, Amount (LCY)Estimated. The amounts and dates are BC’s, but the published feed does not expose which payment settled which invoice (Closed at Date and Closed by Entry No. are not included in the web service). Payments are therefore applied to each supplier’s oldest open invoices first (FIFO) and the interval measured. Dependable in aggregate; an individual supplier’s figure may differ from the actual application.
Payment-speed trend by quarterVendor Ledger EntriesThe same estimate, grouped by the quarter in which the payment was posted. Last 13 quarters.
Not reported here, and why. Each point below was checked against the live company, not assumed.

1. In-house sales-agent commission. Agents are employees, not vendors: they have no vendor account and raise no invoice, so they cannot appear on an AP register. Commission paid is in BC — G/L 21014 Sales Commission — and can be attributed to an agent through the UNITNAME dimension joined to the booking agreement’s salesperson (about 61% of that account’s value carries a unit; the account also holds brokerage commission, separated by the PAYMENTPLAN dimension). What BC does not hold is commission earned but not yet paid: account 21014 is a payment and clearing account — 98% of its movement is debits, with no accrual — so the entitlement exists only in Finance’s manual schedule. Since an agent’s outstanding balance is earned less paid, it cannot be produced from BC and is excluded rather than estimated. Accruing commission per agent in BC would resolve it.

2. Exact payment timing. The published Vendor Ledger Entries service exposes 26 fields; Closed at Date, Closed by Entry No., Closed by Amount and Applies-to ID are not among them, and no other published service carries them (Detailed Vendor Ledger Entries is not published; the Power BI vendor feed exposes four fields). Invoice-to-payment application is therefore unavailable and the payment-speed metrics are a FIFO estimate. Adding those fields to the web service would make them exact.

3. Contract versus variation split. AP invoice lines do carry dimensions — unit (Shortcut Dimension 1) and project (Shortcut Dimension 2) — but nothing that distinguishes contract works from variations. Thirty-three lines mention a variation in the free-text description only, which is not a reliable basis for a figure, so the split is not reported. A dedicated dimension or flag on the invoice line would provide it.

4. Entries with invalid posting dates. Four entries carry dates outside any plausible period and are excluded from the ageing analysis: PPI000424 / PPCM000028 (Vivium Living, dated 2029) and PPI000941 / PPCM000105 (Google, dated 3035). Each is an invoice and matching credit memo that net to nil with no remaining balance, so no supplier balance and no total is affected.
